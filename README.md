# BONS

**BON ODORI NETWORK SERVICE**

BONS is a community-driven hub for discovering, organizing, and participating in bon odori events and festivals across Japan.

## Product direction

- Nationwide coverage: traditional, local, large-scale, DJ, creative, practice, and educational bon odori events.
- Two primary user groups: participants and organizers.
- Open participation: anyone can propose or register an event.
- Independent organizers and communities are first-class participants; the platform is not government-led.
- Participation, learning, and cultural continuity matter more than passive event listings.

- [Product Requirements Document](docs/PRD.md)

## Core experience

1. Organizers register events.
2. Participants discover events through search, map, and calendar.
3. Participants attend and check in.
4. Participants collect stamps, medals, and skill badges.
5. Participants learn dances through reference videos and optional AI-assisted practice assessment.

## Principles

- Preserve regional and stylistic diversity; never define one universal correct dance.
- AI assessment is practice support, not an authoritative cultural judgement.
- Public video sharing is optional and requires explicit consent.
- Keep BONS independently deployable and capable of spinning out by design.
